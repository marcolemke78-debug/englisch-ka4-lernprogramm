Teil 1 - Listening / 8 P

  4. What is he doing right now?
  5. What does he do every weekend?

Teil 3 - Leseverstehen / 8 P

Lies den Text und beantworte die Fragen darunter in ganzen Sätzen.

Lucy is fifteen and she lives in San Francisco. She loves her city, but the weather is often cold and there is a lot of fog in the morning. Lucy is very interested in social media. She has had an Instagram account for two years, and now she has more than 500 followers. Every day she posts photos of the Golden Gate Bridge and the famous cable cars. Her friends think her photos are really catchy. But Lucy is careful: she has never given out her phone number or her address online. "You always have to be careful on the internet," she says.

  1. Where does Lucy live?
  2. How long has she had her Instagram account?
  3. What does she post every day?
  4. Why does Lucy never give out her phone number?

Teil 4 - Grammatik: Zeitformen / 8 P

Setze das Verb in Klammern in der richtigen Zeitform ein (simple present, present progressive oder present perfect).

My name is Emma and I (1) (love) California. Every summer my family (2) (visit) my aunt in San Diego. Look at this photo - here I (3) (surf) for the first time! I (4) (never / be) so happy in my life. My aunt (5) (live) in San Diego for twenty years and she (6) (already / show) me the whole city. Right now we (7) (sit) on the beach and the sun (8) (shine).

Teil 5 - Adjektiv oder Adverb? / 6 P

Setze die richtige Form des Wortes in Klammern ein.

  1. The surfer is very (good).
  2. She rides the waves (perfect).
  3. The ad is really (catchy).
  4. The music plays (loud).
  5. Tom can run very (fast).
  6. This song sounds (good).

Teil 6 - Present perfect / 6 P

Bilde das present perfect (have/has + 3. Form). Achte auf die Signalwörter.

  1. I (just / finish) my homework.
  2. She (never / be) to London.
  3. you ever (eat) sushi?
  4. We (already / see) this film.

since oder for?

Teil 8 - Schreiben / 8 P

Wähle ein Thema (A oder B) und schreibe 5-6 Sätze. Benutze mindestens ein present perfect und mindestens zwei Adjektive.

Thema A: Eine Freundin fragt dich nach deinem Lieblings-Social-Media. Welche App nutzt du? Seit wann? Was machst du dort?
Thema B: Erzähle von einer Reise (echt oder erfunden). Wo warst du? Was hast du dort gesehen und gemacht?

Vorlese-Text für Teil 1 (Listening)

Bitte langsam und deutlich zweimal vorlesen. Zwischen den beiden Malen eine kurze Pause.

"Hi, I am Mike. I have lived in Los Angeles for ten years. I work in the film industry, so I have met some famous actors. Right now I am working on a new movie about surfing. And every weekend I post photos on social media. It is hard work, but I love it."

My favourite app is Instagram. I have used it for about a year. Every day I look at posts from my friends and I have already shared a lot of photos. It is a fun and creative app. But I have never given out my personal information, because that is dangerous.
