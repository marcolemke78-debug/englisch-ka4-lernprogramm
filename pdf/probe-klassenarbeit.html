Teil 1 - Listening / 8 P

Du hörst einen Text über Mike aus Los Angeles. Beantworte die Fragen in ganzen oder kurzen Sätzen.

  1. Where does Mike live?
  2. How long has he lived there?
  3. What is his job?
  4. What is he doing right now?

Teil 3 - Grammatik: Zeitformen / 10 P

Setze das Verb in Klammern in der richtigen Zeitform ein (simple present, present progressive oder present perfect).

My name is Emma and I (1) (love) California. Every summer my family (2) (visit) my aunt in San Diego. Look at this photo - here I (3) (surf) for the first time! I (4) (never / be) so happy in my life. My aunt (5) (live) in San Diego for twenty years and she (6) (already / show) me the whole city.

Teil 4 - Adjektiv oder Adverb? / 4 P

  1. The surfer is very (good).
  2. She rides the waves (perfect).
  3. The ad is really (catchy).
  4. The music plays (loud).

Vorlese-Text für Teil 1 (Listening)

Bitte langsam und deutlich zweimal vorlesen. Zwischen den beiden Malen eine kurze Pause.

"Hi, I am Mike. I have lived in Los Angeles for ten years. I work in the film industry, so I have met some famous actors. Right now I am working on a new movie. It is hard work, but I love it."

Teil 1 - Listening (8 P)

1 He lives in Los Angeles. · 2 He has lived there for ten years. · 3 He works in the film industry. · 4 He is working on a new movie (right now).

Teil 2 - Wortschatz (8 P)

1 fog · 2 to settle · 3 catchy · 4 outdated · 5 Anhänger/in (Follower) · 6 dauern/andauern · 7 Konto · 8 Seilbahn

Teil 3 - Zeitformen (10 P)

1 love (Fakt) · 2 visits (Every summer, family = it) · 3 am surfing (Foto-Beschreibung, jetzt) · 4 have never been (Erfahrung bis jetzt) · 5 has lived (for twenty years - present perfect) · 6 has already shown (already, 3. Form von show)

Teil 4 - Adjektiv/Adverb (4 P)

1 good · 2 perfectly · 3 catchy · 4 loudly

Teil 5 - Sätze bilden (4 P)

1 Have you ever tried Mexican food?
2 Mike is working on a new film at the moment.

Teil 6 - Schreiben (6 P, Beispiel)

My favourite app is Instagram. I have used it for about a year. Every day I look at posts from my friends and I have already shared a lot of photos. It is a fun and creative app. But I have never given out my personal information, because that is dangerous.

Bewertung Schreiben: present perfect korrekt (2 P), zwei Adjektive (2 P), Aufgabe inhaltlich erfüllt & verständlich (2 P).
