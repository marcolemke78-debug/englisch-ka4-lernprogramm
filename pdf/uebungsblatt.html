B - Simple present oder present progressive?

Setze das Verb in Klammern in der richtigen Form ein.

  1. Every morning Tom (walk) to school.
  2. Listen! The baby (cry).
  3. Sandra (surf) every weekend.
  4. Look - right now she (ride) a big wave!
  5. My brother usually (play) football on Saturdays.
  6. Be quiet, please. I (do) my homework at the moment.
